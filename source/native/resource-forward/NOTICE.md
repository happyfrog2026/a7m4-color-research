# Forward resource helper provenance

This independent forward-only helper adapts the MIT-licensed resource-prefix
COW and mapping proof mechanism retained in
`a7m4/toolbox/restore_helpers/source/resource-delta/native/prefix_delta.c`.
The restoration snapshot and its original-backup authority are unchanged.

The private-view protection technique traces through PF2 to the upstream
`portrait_frames.c` research in https://github.com/QAQLeonard/a7c2-fl23-research.
Retain LICENSE.MIT and LICENSE.A7C2 with redistributed source copies.
The compact SHA implementation is the existing MIT resource-sha256.h.

Only allowlisted UXC files can receive source-bound windows and append data.
There is no truncate, rename, automatic inverse, AP/CP/settings/NOR/WBI writer.
Generated headers and ELF objects contain private source/target bytes and stay
in the user's local job; they are excluded from code and artifact releases.

Offline synthetic validation and historical research do not constitute physical
acceptance of this new product helper.
